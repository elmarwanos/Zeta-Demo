The second door
into Pakistan.

For decades, almost all of the country's international traffic arrived by sea at Karachi. Zeta built Pakistan's first terrestrial cable landing station on the Pak-China optical fibre, opening a land route from the north that does not depend on the subsea cables in the south.

That station, together with an LDI licence and interconnects to every major operator, is why carriers and cloud providers route through Zeta rather than around it.
